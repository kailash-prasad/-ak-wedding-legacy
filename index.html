A LETTER TO GO WITH YOUR GIFT

For the people
who made our day.

A & KWITH LOVE AND THANKS

Dear family and friends,

Thank you for being with us as we got married. Looking around at Vedic Village and seeing people we love from so many parts of our lives in one place is something we’ll remember for a long time. You made our wedding in Kolkata feel like home.

When we think back on those two days, we’ll remember the colour of the Mehendi, the music and dancing at the Sangeet, the laughter at Gaye Holud, and the warmth of your blessings as we got married. Just as much, we’ll remember the smaller things: the hugs, the conversations between celebrations, and the way our two families had time to get to know each other.

We know that being there took time and effort. Some of you travelled a long way, and many of you made room in busy lives to spend those days with us. Thank you for showing up, for celebrating so wholeheartedly, and for making us feel so loved.

A little gift, with our thanks

We hope this gift brings back a happy moment from those two days. It’s a small way of saying how much it meant to share the beginning of our married life with you.

We made this keepsake so we could share the photographs and come back to these memories together. If there’s a moment you remember that we might have missed, we’d love to hear it. Leave it in the guestbook for us to read together.

With love and gratitude,

Aditya & Kailash
